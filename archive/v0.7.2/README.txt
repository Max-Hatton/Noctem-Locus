Astronomy Companion v0.7.2

Open “Astronomy Companion v0.7.2.html” in Edge or Chrome.

New in v0.7.2:
- Attach multiple photos to observation log entries.
- Photos are stored offline in IndexedDB rather than localStorage.
- Thumbnail gallery in each observation.
- Click a thumbnail for a full-screen view.
- Remove individual photos while editing an entry.
- Deleting an observation also deletes its stored photos.
- CSV exports include photo count.

No internet connection is required.
