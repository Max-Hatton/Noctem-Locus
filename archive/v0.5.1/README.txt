Astronomy Companion v0.5.1 — Smart App Safe portable build

RUN IT
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click "Astronomy Companion v0.5.1.html".
3. It opens in your default browser and runs locally/offline.

NEW IN v0.5.1
- Rotatable interactive sky map
- Drag the Facing slider from 0–359 degrees
- Quick N / E / S / W facing buttons
- Rotate left/right in 15 degree steps
- [ and ] keyboard shortcuts rotate the map 15 degrees
- The direction at the top of the map is labeled FACING

EXAMPLE
If you are physically facing south, click S. South will move to the top of the map, east will be on your left, and west will be on your right — matching the sky in front of you.

All v0.5 features remain offline and self-contained. No executable, launcher script, fetch call, or external web resource is required.
