Astronomy Companion v0.8

Open "Astronomy Companion v0.8.html" in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.

v0.8 adds Horizon View while preserving the original All-Sky map.

Horizon View controls:
- Click and drag the sky to look around.
- Mouse wheel changes field of view / zoom.
- Arrow keys nudge facing/elevation by 5 degrees.
- N/E/S/W buttons instantly face a cardinal direction.
- Center selected points the virtual camera at the selected object.
- Face scope centers the view on the telescope reticle when telescope pointing is available.

The app remains a self-contained offline HTML file. Observation photos remain stored in the browser's IndexedDB for this local file origin.
