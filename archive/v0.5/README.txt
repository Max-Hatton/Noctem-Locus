Astronomy Companion v0.5 — Smart App Safe portable build

RUN IT
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click "Astronomy Companion v0.5.html".
3. It opens in your default browser and runs locally/offline.

NEW IN v0.5
- Interactive all-sky horizon map
- Bright stars, constellation guide lines, solar-system bodies, and all Messier objects
- Click plotted objects for live Alt/Az and visibility details
- Step the sky backward/forward by 10 minutes or 1 hour
- Layer toggles for solar system, stars, constellations, Messier objects, and labels
- Shortcut: S opens Sky Map; N toggles night vision
- Telescope reticle channel reserved for future IMU/encoder integration

NOTES
- The outer circle is the horizon; the center is the zenith.
- North is at the top and east is to the right.
- Everything remains embedded in the HTML; there are no external scripts or network requests.
- Browser localStorage behavior for file:// pages varies by browser/path. If settings do not carry from an older HTML filename, re-enter them or use Export Settings in the older version as a backup.
